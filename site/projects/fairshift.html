01 / THE STORY

The problem worth solving.

Problem

Workers and employers need understandable records of shifts, overtime, breaks and disagreements over calculated wages.

Solution

Separate worker and employer dashboards, attendance state transitions, sector-specific pay rules, discrepancy detection and structured dispute resolution.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Constraints & choices.

Engineering considerations derived from the documented implementation.

Engineering challenge

Keeping attendance transitions, authorization and integer-paise calculations consistent.

Trade-offs

Database transactions and row-level security centralize correctness constraints. The documented local tests leave production readiness unverified.

Limitations & verification

This is a hackathon prototype; not a live payroll or payment-processing service.
