01 / THE STORY

The problem worth solving.

Problem

Procedural runners are deceptively complex: movement, train geometry, collisions and generated obstacles must stay synchronized at increasingly high speeds.

Solution

A browser-based 3D endless runner with three-lane movement, train-roof traversal, dynamic environments, collectible systems, powers and persistent progression.

02 / ENGINEERING

Under the surface.

Implementation

Three.js, TypeScript, WebGL and Vite, with procedural track generation, collision systems, Web Audio, and automated gameplay regression tests.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Constraints & choices.

Engineering considerations derived from the documented implementation.

Engineering challenge

Keeping movement, obstacles and train geometry synchronized as speed increases.

Trade-offs

Procedural generation creates variety. Solvability and collision behavior need gameplay regression evidence across generated sequences.

Limitations & verification

Metro Runner is in private development. No playable deployment or source repository is linked from this portfolio; visuals are original conceptual art.
