01 / THE STORY

The problem worth solving.

Problem

Autoregressive inference retains key/value states that grow with context length. Reducing retained states without collapsing recall requires careful selection and correct decoding.

Solution

Built and evaluated an observation-guided cache retention pipeline, comparing record-aware selection against sliding-window, StreamingLLM-style and static H2O-style baselines on synthetic long-context questions.

02 / ENGINEERING

Under the surface.

Implementation

PyTorch + Hugging Face Transformers on Qwen2.5-1.5B. Exact post-decode KV budget enforcement, absolute RoPE positions, reproducible seeds and independently checked benchmark artifacts.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Constraints & choices.

Engineering considerations derived from the documented implementation.

Engineering challenge

Enforcing a post-decode cache budget while preserving absolute positions and recall.

Trade-offs

Retaining fewer KV states lowers retained-cache size. The documented recall difference and unresolved consistency issues constrain the result.

Limitations & verification

Experimental result only. Peak-memory savings and general long-context reliability have not been established. The repository documents unresolved consistency limitations.
