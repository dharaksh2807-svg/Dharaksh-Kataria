01 / THE STORY

The problem worth solving.

Problem

Notes, slides, exam papers and study tools often live in disconnected folders and apps. Finding the right material — and figuring out what matters — takes time students do not have.

Solution

An independent academic workspace organizing 67 listed subjects across six semesters, with previous-year question analysis, topic heatmaps, an embedded compiler, formula tools, active recall and subject-aware AI support.

02 / ENGINEERING

Under the surface.

Implementation

Modular vanilla JavaScript, offline-first PWA caching, Firebase Auth / Firestore modules, cloud document integration and a BYOK multi-model tutor. Some cloud features may vary by production rollout.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Constraints & choices.

Engineering considerations derived from the documented implementation.

Engineering challenge

Keeping study resources available offline while integrating cloud-backed accounts and AI.

Trade-offs

Offline content access reduces network dependence. Account, document and tutor features still depend on their configured services.
