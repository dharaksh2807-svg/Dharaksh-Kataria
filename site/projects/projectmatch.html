01 / THE STORY

The problem worth solving.

Problem

Student teams commonly form through existing networks rather than aligning around complementary skills, time availability and project needs.

Solution

A skills-based collaboration and hackathon-discovery platform with weighted compatibility, role applications, project workflows, messaging and multi-model AI tooling.

02 / ENGINEERING

Under the surface.

Implementation

Next.js + TypeScript, PostgreSQL with 28 Prisma models, NextAuth, provider SDKs, test infrastructure with Vitest and Playwright.
